BlokArcade Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

BlokArcade does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and does not connect to the internet. The only data it keeps is your high score for each game, which is stored on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes that data.

If you have questions, contact blakeg86@gmail.com.
